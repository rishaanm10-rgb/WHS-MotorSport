Privacy & Cookie Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

The short version: we only collect what you type into our sign-up form, we use it only to send you club updates, we never sell it, and this website doesn't use cookies, analytics or ad trackers.

Who we are

The WHS Motorsport & STEM Club is a student organization at Winchester High School in Winchester, Massachusetts. This website is run by club members. It is not an official website of Winchester High School or Winchester Public Schools.

Questions about this policy go to whsmotorsportandstemclub@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us through the Join the Club sign-up form:

  • Your full name
  • Your grade
  • The area of the club you're interested in
  • Your email address

We don't ask for your phone number, home address, birthday, photos or passwords. Browsing the site without signing up gives us no information about you.

How we use it

We use sign-up information only to:

  • Send you club updates, such as meeting times, projects and events
  • Plan meetings and group members by interest

We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or send you anything unrelated to the club.

Who can see it

Sign-ups go to the club's Google account, either into a Google Form or as an email to the club's Gmail. Only club officers and the club's faculty advisor can see them. Google stores them on our behalf under Google's Privacy Policy.

We would only share sign-up information with anyone else if Winchester Public Schools staff need it for school supervision of the club, or if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep sign-ups for the current school year. We delete them at the end of each school year, except for members who are still active in the club, and sooner if you ask us to.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, fix or delete your information, or stop sending you updates, at any time. Email whsmotorsportandstemclub@gmail.com and we'll take care of it within 30 days. Parents and guardians can make these requests for their student.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies. It also doesn't use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising tools or browser storage to follow you around the site or the internet.

Because we don't use any non-essential cookies, there is no cookie banner. If we ever add something that uses cookies, like visitor analytics or an embedded video, we'll update this policy first and ask for your permission where that's required.

Outside services this site uses

Like most websites, ours loads a few things from other companies. When your browser downloads them, those companies receive standard technical information that comes with every web request, such as your IP address and browser type. We don't receive or control that information.

Students under 13

This site is meant for high school students, families, staff and community members, and it isn't directed at children under 13. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before signing up. If we learn we've received information from a child under 13 without a parent's permission, we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date at the top.
