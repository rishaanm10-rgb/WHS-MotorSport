About the club

We bridge the gap between a passion for motorsport and practical STEM education.

Whether you follow F1, NASCAR, GT3 or endurance racing, or you just like figuring out how things work, this is a place to talk racing, dig into the physics behind it, and build real things with your hands.

Members work on sim racing and lap time analysis, design CO2 cars for STEM Racing, and are building toward a full sim setup this year. Every experience level is welcome.

  • Engineering & Fabrication

    Design parts, then cut, sand, print and assemble them. Learn by building and fixing.

  • Aerodynamics & Design

    Shape bodies to cut drag, test ideas in CAD and on track, and see why the fast shape wins.

  • Telemetry & Data Science

    Pull lap data from the sim, compare traces, and find exactly where time is gained or lost.

  • Race Strategy & Management

    Plan pit windows and tire calls, run projects on a timeline, and manage a team budget.

      Divisions & projects

      Pick a project, join the crew working on it, and bring your own ideas.

      Active

      Sim Racing & Telemetry

      Race on the sim, then study the data to find where every tenth of a second went.

      • Weekly hot-lap challenges
      • Comparing speed, brake and throttle traces
      • Building toward a full sim rig this year
      Active

      STEM Racing CO2 Cars

      Design, model and build CO2-powered cars to compete in STEM Racing.

      • CAD design and body shaping
      • Weight and drag trade-offs
      • Build, test and refine
      Active

      Aerodynamic Testing

      Test body shapes and wings to see how airflow changes drag and downforce.

      • Smoke and string flow visualization
      • Comparing designs head to head
      • Recording results for the CO2 car team
      Planned

      RC Car Design & Mods

      Tune and modify RC cars: gearing, suspension, weight and setup changes.

      • Setup changes and timed runs
      • 3D-printed parts
      • Logging what each change does
      Planned

      EV & Kart Builds

      A longer-term goal: a student-built electric kart, from frame to battery.

      • Scoping the build and budget
      • Electrical safety basics
      • Depends on sponsors and space
      Active

      Race Weekend Breakdowns

      Talk through real race weekends: strategy calls, pit stops and why they worked.

      • Strategy debates after big races
      • Tire and fuel planning
      • Predicting the calls before they happen
